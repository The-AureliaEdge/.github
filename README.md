## Questions buyers ask

**Do we need to replace our tools?** Rarely. We connect to the systems you already use through integrations and CRM improvements, and we assess what should connect before agreeing the scope.

**Will we get the same results as the examples?** Outcomes depend on the process, the data, the integrations and how your team adopts the workflow. That is why every sprint starts with a baseline and agreed success measures.

**How is progress measured?** Saved time, response speed, data quality, adoption and reliability. The first workflow is evaluated before anything expands.

**What should I include in my enquiry?** The workflow you want to improve, the tools your team uses, and the point where work slows down. For example: how leads move from your inbox to the CRM, or how the weekly report gets assembled.
