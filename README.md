## Growth systems we build

### Speed to lead

Leads cool fast. A form submission that waits until tomorrow usually goes to whoever replied first. We build the system that answers in minutes, not hours.

- **Capture** every enquiry from forms, inbox, chat and social in one place, with the source recorded.
- **Qualify and route** each lead to the right person or process, without a dispatcher.
- **Reply in minutes** with a drafted or sent first response that offers a time instead of asking for more information.
- **Update the CRM** automatically, so nothing lives only in someone's inbox.
- **Follow up** on a schedule, with a human approval step where you want one.

What we measure: time to first response, leads that reach the CRM, and calls booked.

### AEO: get cited by AI, not just ranked by Google

Buyers now ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews for recommendations. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) makes sure the answer includes you. We pair it with technical SEO so you also rank in classic search. [Full service details ↗](https://www.theaureliaedge.com/services#aio-seo)

- **Visibility audit** across search engines and AI assistants, so you know where you stand today.
- **One page per service**, written around the words clients type, not internal job titles.
- **Content an AI assistant can quote correctly**: services, coverage and process stated in a form assistants can lift without inventing the missing parts.
- **Structured data, metadata, Core Web Vitals** and the rest of the technical groundwork.
- **Lead-source tracking**, so you can see which pages bring work in.

What we measure: search and AI visibility, and organic enquiries by source.

### AI agents for the follow-through

Once leads arrive and get answered, the rest of the work still has to happen. Our agents [triage inboxes, draft replies, run research, generate content and update your systems](https://www.theaureliaedge.com/services#ai-agents), working alongside your team around the clock.
